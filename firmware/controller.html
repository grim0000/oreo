Walks upright. With table-edge detection on it looks down at the floor only when needed (after walking a stretch, after turning, or when the camera sees the floor ending) and won't walk anywhere it hasn't seen floor. Curves gently around things it sees coming (Fwd-L / Fwd-R) and wanders a little on open floor. In the dark the camera switches off by itself and it goes on ToF + IMU (slower); it steers clear of dark places like under a bed. Stops under 12 cm, slowly sweeps its head across ~150° like a radar (a reading every ~2°) and turns toward the middle of the widest gap it fits through; after two turns the same way it prefers the other side. It only backs up in a dead end (or after getting stuck). With no floor that way or in a tight space, it turns on the spot like the D-pad . Every turn is checked with the gyro and every walk with the camera: if it slides in place, can't turn, tilts or bumps, it backs up and tries a different direction (never the one that just failed).

Head (pan / tilt)
Drag in the square to aim the head: left / right = pan, up / down = tilt. Let go and it goes back to the middle - with Hold on it stays where you leave it.
Steady Gait
A smooth trot: it eases into the stride and out of it, and keeps its heading with the gyro (it shortens one side's strides when it drifts). Hold to walk. Legs move: on the floor only.
-
Level-Gait
The steady gait with the body kept parallel to the ground: the IMU measures the tilt while it walks and slowly bends the front knees up / back knees down until it's level (it remembers for the next walk). Hold to walk. Legs move: on the floor only.
-
Balanced Walk
A proper diagonal trot with every leg doing the same thing: front and back legs lift the same, the hips swing symmetrically, the feet only lift while swinging forward. The IMU keeps the body level and walking straight. Hold to walk. Legs move: on the floor only.

Talk through the robot

This browser only lets a secure (https) page use the microphone, and the controller is a plain http page.

Best: switch to secure mode. The same controller opens again as https:// and comes straight back to RC - then this mic button works right here. The first time the browser says the connection isn't private: it's the robot's own certificate - tap Advanced → Proceed (once).

Android Chrome, right here in RC: open chrome://flags, find Insecure origins treated as secure, add http://<robot ip>, set it to Enabled and relaunch Chrome. Then this mic button works directly.

The robot isn't on a WiFi network

You're on the robot's own hotspot. Joining a WiFi network gives your phone its internet back and a longer range, but the robot must search for it - and while it searches, the hotspot lags.

Away from home? Turn on your phone's Personal Hotspot (iPhone: switch on Maximize Compatibility), pick it above and connect: the robot joins your phone - RC runs smoothly that way (the robot's own hotspot is slower with iPhones). The robot remembers it and the OLED shows its address there.

Joining moves the hotspot to the router's channel: your phone may drop off for a few seconds - reconnect to "Oreo" if it does.
