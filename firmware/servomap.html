Servo mapping

Tell Oreo which servo board channel (0-15 on the PCA9685) each servo is plugged into. Use Find a servo to wiggle a channel and see which leg or head servo moves, then pick that channel for it. Changes are saved at once. Do this with Oreo lifted (legs free), then calibrate on the calibration page.
